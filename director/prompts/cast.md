# The cast (Soothing Bots' sixteen robots: heads with floating hands)

Households: the family home (Dan and Nora with Ivy and Teddy; next door Constance and her son Knox);
Lou's Diner (Grandpa Lou, Grandma Pearl front of house; Sterling's food truck parks across the road;
Murphy eats lunch there); the big house (Auntie Gigi and Auntie Vera with their son Benny); down the
street (Ray and his daughter Hattie; Frankie on her porch on the corner).

- Teddy (boy, almost 4, soprano): a pocket professor; knows every dinosaur and fact, scared to try
  what he has not read about, corrects grown-ups, takes things literally. Says "Actually...". The
  "I" of most songs: his facts solve the problem, his worries make the tension.
- Ivy (9, alto): Teddy's big sister, cool, a born negotiator, cuts corners, embarrassed by her family.
  Says "Trust me."
- Dan (Dad, coach voice): PE teacher, endless energy, too competitive, pushes too hard. Says "One more
  lap!" Learns to let go.
- Nora (Mum, alto): the family doctor, calm, fixes anything, cannot switch off. Says "Let me take a
  look." Learns some things need a hug, not fixing.
- Lou (Grandpa, elder voice): owns Lou's Diner, famous pancakes, stingy ("Extra syrup? That's extra."),
  hates change; secretly pays for Murphy's soup on rainy days. Says "That's extra." Learns what you
  give away comes back.
- Pearl (Grandma, alto): the funniest person at every table, cannot say no, tells little white lies
  ("Delicious!"). Says "Of course, darling!" Lou's opposite: he keeps, she gives.
- Hattie (girl, 4, soprano): Teddy's best friend, brave, takes every dare, acts before she thinks.
  Says "Hold my juice!"
- Ray (Hattie's dad, tenor): an estate agent and relentless optimist, always late, lives in next year.
  Says "This one's got potential!" Learns the best day is this one.
- Gigi (Auntie, soprano): a toy designer who arrives with something huge, spoils the kids, spends on a
  whim. Says "Who wants a surprise?" Learns time beats stuff.
- Vera (Auntie, alto): a calm trader, plans everything, overthinks, secretly the best dancer. Says
  "What's the risk?" Learns some things are worth the leap.
- Benny (6, soprano): the kindest kid in town, believes everything (Knox's tall tales), gives his
  things away. Says "Really? Wow!"
- Knox (5, soprano, a rival): the kid next door, schemes and dares, a sore loser who wants friends.
  Says "Bet you can't!"
- Constance (alto, a rival): the neighbour with a rule for everything, a superb baker, Pearl's bake-off
  rival. Says "There's a rule for that."
- Sterling (crooner, a rival): the food-truck showman across from the diner; Lou taught him
  everything and he will not admit it. Says "Ta-da!"
- Murphy (basso, the unlucky one): the delivery driver; if it can go wrong it does, and he still
  delivers; will not step on cracks. Says "Just my luck."
- Frankie (robo voice): the retired sports commentator calling the street like a grand final from her
  porch. Says "And they're off!"

Rivals are rivals, not villains: each has a soft spot and a way back. Story recipe: one problem
(brought by Knox, Constance or Sterling, Murphy's luck, Hattie's dares, Gigi's big box or Frankie's
cheering), one weakness put to the test, two strengths that only work together, and a grown-up layer
underneath (letting go, saying no, enjoying today).
