# The cast (Soothing Bots' sixteen robots: heads with floating hands)

Each robot is called by the colour of its face (Green, White, Gold...), so the audience can tell
them apart at a glance. A name is the Capitalised word; in lower case it is just a colour.

Households: the family home (Orange and White with Red and Green; next door Lilac and her son Black);
Gold's Diner (Grandpa Gold, Grandma Pink front of house; Purple's food truck parks across the road;
Silver eats lunch there); the big house (Auntie Blue and Auntie Gray with their son Navy); down the
street (Copper and his daughter Jade; Yellow on her porch on the corner).

- Green (boy, almost 4, soprano): a pocket professor; knows every dinosaur and fact, scared to try
  what he has not read about, corrects grown-ups, takes things literally. Says "Actually...". The
  lead of most songs (singing "we" with the others): his facts solve the problem, his worries make the tension.
- Red (9, alto): Green's big sister, cool, a born negotiator, cuts corners, embarrassed by her family.
  Says "Trust me."
- Orange (Dad, coach voice): PE teacher, endless energy, too competitive, pushes too hard. Says "One more
  lap!" Learns to let go.
- White (Mum, alto): the family doctor, calm, fixes anything, cannot switch off. Says "Let me take a
  look." Learns some things need a hug, not fixing.
- Gold (Grandpa, elder voice): owns Gold's Diner, famous pancakes, stingy ("Extra syrup? That's extra."),
  hates change; secretly pays for Silver's soup on rainy days. Says "That's extra." Learns what you
  give away comes back.
- Pink (Grandma, alto): the funniest person at every table, cannot say no, tells little white lies
  ("Delicious!"). Says "Of course, darling!" Gold's opposite: he keeps, she gives.
- Jade (girl, 4, soprano): Green's best friend, brave, takes every dare, acts before she thinks.
  Says "Hold my juice!"
- Copper (Jade's dad, tenor): an estate agent and relentless optimist, always late, lives in next year.
  Says "This one's got potential!" Learns the best day is this one.
- Blue (Auntie, soprano): a toy designer who arrives with something huge, spoils the kids, spends on a
  whim. Says "Who wants a surprise?" Learns time beats stuff.
- Gray (Auntie, alto): a calm trader, plans everything, overthinks, secretly the best dancer. Says
  "What's the risk?" Learns some things are worth the leap.
- Navy (6, soprano): the kindest kid in town, believes everything (Black's tall tales), gives his
  things away. Says "Really? Wow!"
- Black (5, soprano, a rival): the kid next door, schemes and dares, a sore loser who wants friends.
  Says "Bet you can't!"
- Lilac (alto, a rival): the neighbour with a rule for everything, a superb baker, Pink's bake-off
  rival. Says "There's a rule for that."
- Purple (crooner, a rival): the food-truck showman across from the diner; Gold taught him
  everything and he will not admit it. Says "Ta-da!"
- Silver (basso, the unlucky one): the delivery driver; if it can go wrong it does, and he still
  delivers; will not step on cracks. Says "Just my luck."
- Yellow (robo voice): the retired sports commentator calling the street like a grand final from her
  porch. Says "And they're off!"

Rivals are rivals, not villains: each has a soft spot and a way back. Story recipe: one problem
(brought by Black, Lilac or Purple, Silver's luck, Jade's dares, Blue's big box or Yellow's
cheering), one weakness put to the test, two strengths that only work together, and a grown-up layer
underneath (letting go, saying no, enjoying today).
