# A finished story song, for the craft (Soothing Bots' "Just a Box", by the app's author)

Premise: Gigi brings home the biggest toy in the shop. Lead Teddy, pal Ivy (the echo), grown-up Gigi,
bridge tender. The irony, rooted in the premise: the toy designer's dragon plays the game by itself,
so the kids play with its box; her motor turns the box into another toy that plays without them; the
best toy is the auntie on the floor (chorus 3's turned line), and her next surprise is an empty box
with her inside (the outro). Grown-up layer: time beats stuff.

Lines are what is sung; `(Name)` marks a line a character sings themself (the event's `singer`),
otherwise the lead sings.

- intro: `Knock, knock at the door >> Who's there?` | `{Gigi:preen} and a big box >> Box who?`
- verse1: (Gigi) `Who wants a surprise? >> Depends!` | `[Oh,] A dragon, shiny new >> Fancy!` |
  `It roars! I want a go! >> Dibs!` | `It even plays for you >> Handy!`
- verse1b: `The dragon hogs the fun >> Rude!` | `[And] I sit there, getting bored >> Same!` |
  (Ivy) `Trust me: it's a ship!` | `Actually, it's cardboard >> Boring!`
- chorus1: `Just a cardboard box! >> Or is it?` | `[Oh,] It's a rocket, painted blue >> Blast off!` |
  `Just a cardboard box! >> Or is it?` | `[And] A ship, a cave, a zoo >> Ahoy!`
- verse2: `{Gigi} grabs the dragon >> Pass!` | `[Look,] The dragon does a flip >> Big deal!` |
  `{Gigi:peek} knocks: Let me in! >> Busy!` | (Ivy) `Kids only on the ship!`
- verse2b: `{Gigi:wink} adds a motor >> Uh-oh!` | `[And] Lights that flash and beep >> Too much!` |
  `It goes zoom, zoom, zoom >> Not cool!` | `[Oh,] {Ivy:gasp} lands in a heap >> I'm fine!`
- chorus2: the same four lines as chorus1 (the replies may change).
- bridge (Gigi, her own "I", no replies): `I want to be the fun one` | `So I buy things big and new` |
  `A toy that plays without you` | `Leaves nothing left to do`
- bridge2 (Gigi; one line the lead's): `I take the motor out` | `I'm far too big to fit` |
  (Teddy) `Actually, there's room!` | `I squeeze in, bit by bit`
- chorus3: `Just a cardboard box! >> Or is it?` | `[Oh,] It's a rocket, painted blue >> Blast off!` |
  `Just a cardboard box! >> Or is it?` | (Gigi) `And I'm the dragon, too >> Run!`
- outro: `Just a cardboard box! >> Or is it?` | `Knock, knock at the door >> Who now?` |
  `An empty cardboard box >> Cheap!` | `[Oh,] {Gigi:boing} pops out the top >> Surprise!`

What makes it work: every line is a concrete picture in 4-7 sung syllables; lines 2 and 4 of a stanza
rhyme (new/you, ship/flip); the chorus's Y and the turned line rhyme with its X (blue/zoo/too); the
echo's replies are the pal's one-to-four-word reactions, never names, never in the bridges; characters
sing their own catchphrases; the bridges are the grown-up's quiet confession; the turned line flips
the chorus to land the irony; the outro calls back the intro's first line in a changed world.
